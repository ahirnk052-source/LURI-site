About Lokbharti University

Overview

Lokbharti University for Rural Innovation (LURI) focuses on rural development through education, innovation, and community engagement.

Vision

To create a self-reliant rural society through education and sustainability.

Mission

  • Quality rural education
  • Research & innovation
  • Skill development
  • Modern + traditional learning

Key Features

  • Rural innovation focus
  • Practical learning
  • Community connection
  • Eco-friendly campus

Academic Approach

Hands-on learning with real-life rural experience and fieldwork.

Why Special?

  • Village development focus
  • Agriculture support
  • Social responsibility
  • Sustainable living
