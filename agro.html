B.Voc Agro Proccesing

About Course

B.Voc Agro Processing is a skill-based 3-year degree course that focuses on processing agricultural products into useful and market-ready items.

20% સૈદ્ધાંતિક(થિયરી) + 30% મલ્ટીમીડિયા + 50% પ્રાયોગિક (પ્રેક્ટિકલ/ઈન્ટર્નશીપ)

Subjects / Skills

  • Food Processing Technology
  • Food Preservation Techniques (drying, freezing, canning)
  • Packaging Technology
  • Food Safety & Hygiene
  • Entrepreneurship Development (starting your own business)

Career Options

  • Production Supervisor (Food Industry)
  • Dairy Industry Worker / Supervisor
  • Food Safety Officer
  • Research Assistant (Food / Agriculture field)
